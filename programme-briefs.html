Programme Brief - Roots & Rise
Mentorship Programme
Roots
& Rise.

A structured mentorship programme for young people across Uganda - connecting potential with direction.

"You cannot rise without roots. We build both."

Programme Overview

Roots & Rise connects young Ugandans aged 15-25 across Uganda with skilled mentors who commit to regular, meaningful engagement. It is not a curriculum - it is a relationship.

The programme runs in structured 6-month cycles. Each participant is matched individually with a mentor whose professional background aligns with their aspirations. Sessions are held weekly or bi-weekly, with both in-person and remote options.

Programme Objectives
Help participants articulate and pursue career direction
Build genuine confidence in their own worth and capability
Provide access to professional networks and opportunities
Develop practical communication and planning skills
How It Works - 3 Phases
01
Match & Meet

Each participant is matched with a mentor whose experience aligns with their goals. The relationship begins with a structured intake conversation to establish expectations and direction.

02
Build & Explore

Regular sessions over 6 months - focused on real challenges, decisions, and direction. No rigid curriculum. Honest conversation and consistent attention.

03
Rise & Ripple

Graduates are invited to give back as peer supporters and programme ambassadors - completing the ripple cycle.

Who It Serves
Age Range
15 - 25 years
Location
Across Uganda
Intake Cycle
6-month cohorts
Focus Areas
All career paths welcome
Expected Outcomes
Clear personal and professional direction by programme end
Demonstrated increase in self-confidence and decision-making
Active progression toward education or career goals

Programme Brief - Diane Health
Community Health Programme
Diane Health
Programme.

Primary health education delivered directly into schools and communities across Uganda - because wellness is not a privilege.

"Wellness is not a privilege. It is the ground every person stands on."

Programme Overview

The Diane Health Programme brings primary health education directly into communities and schools across Uganda that have limited access to formal healthcare. It is named for Diane - Harry Maurice's sister - who walked into these communities and asked how she could help.

This is not a treatment programme. It is a primary education programme. Its goal is to equip communities with the knowledge and local champions they need to make better health decisions independently.

Programme Objectives
Build practical health literacy in underserved communities
Reduce preventable illness through primary health education
Open conversations around mental and emotional wellbeing
Train local health champions for self-sustaining impact
Delivery Model - 3 Stages
01
Community Identification

We partner with local leaders and school heads to identify communities with limited health resources. Engagement starts with listening - not prescribing.

02
In-Community Sessions

Small, accessible sessions in halls, schools, and open spaces across Uganda - covering nutrition, hygiene, mental wellness, and first aid basics. Designed for all ages and backgrounds.

03
Champion Training

Community members are trained to carry health conversations forward independently - building self-sustaining local health education that outlasts the programme team's visits.

Who It Serves
Target
All ages, all genders
Location
Across Uganda
Session Length
2-3 hours per session
Priority Areas
Low clinic-access zones
Topics Covered
Nutrition and everyday food choices
Hygiene practices and disease prevention
Mental and emotional wellbeing
First aid basics and when to seek care
Request a Diane Health session Schools, community leaders, and organisations across Uganda can request a session. We work around your schedule.
