Our Story

Harry Maurice came to Uganda for a sports camp. Between sessions, he noticed young people with real potential. He had no plan. He simply chose to show up for them, consistently.

His mentorship touched 20 young people across Uganda - including two named Fatu and Samuel. They were so shaped by what his presence did for them that they built this foundation in his name. It is their tribute to him.

His sister Diane also showed up - and her commitment to community health gave shape to the Diane Health Programme, named for the presence she brought.

What We Believe
Ugandans are capable of building Uganda. Outside support equips - it does not replace.
Every young person carries something significant. Our work helps them see it.
Consistent presence builds more than grand gestures. We show up, repeatedly.
The ripple effect takes time. We are building for the generation ahead.
