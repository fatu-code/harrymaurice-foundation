Uganda · Where it all began
Our Story

It started with
a baseball camp.

Harry Maurice came to Uganda for a baseball camp. He was not there to build anything. Between sessions, he simply noticed young people with potential, and he showed up for them - again and again, without agenda.

His mentorship touched 20 young people - including two named Fatu and Samuel. What happened next was entirely their idea. They built this foundation.

"Where you come from does not limit what you can become. With the right support, you can stand confidently alongside anyone in the world."

- The philosophy behind Harry Maurice Foundation
