Become a Mentor

Share a few hours.
Change a direction.

The Roots & Rise programme connects young Ugandans with mentors who commit to regular, genuine conversations. You do not need to be an expert - you need to show up and listen.

We match mentors to participants based on career direction and personality. The time commitment is 2–4 hours per month, for a minimum of 6 months.

Not sure where to start?
Take the 3-question Mentor Match quiz to find out which kid you'd be matched with.
Partner with Us

Organisations who
believe in potential.

If your organisation aligns with the values of the Harry Maurice Foundation, we want to explore what a partnership could look like. Formal, informal, in-kind - we are open to what makes sense.

Institutional Partners
Universities, NGOs, and government bodies who want to formally support programme delivery and reach.
Corporate Partners
Businesses that provide funding, mentors, internships, or skills-based volunteering to the programme's participants.
Community Partners
Schools, churches, and local organisations who host Diane Health sessions or connect us with young people who need support.
