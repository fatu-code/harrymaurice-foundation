Personal Story

How one afternoon
changed Emmanuel's direction.

Emmanuel had been thinking about medicine for years. But thinking and believing are different things. It wasn't until a mentor sat with him - for a full afternoon - that the belief finally landed.

"Uganda needs doctors like you, Emmanuel. You can do this."

He is now studying pre-medicine and volunteers at a clinic twice a week. He says the hardest part wasn't the work. It was believing it was possible.
