The story behind
the name.

One man noticed a group of young people. Everything else followed.

Uganda · The baseball camp
The Beginning

He came for a game.
He never planned any of this.

Harry Maurice - an American with a love for baseball - arrived in Uganda for a sports camp. He was not there to build anything. He had no plan, no mission, no foundation in mind.

A group of young people. Engaged, hungry, capable. Not waiting to be rescued - waiting to be noticed. Harry noticed.

He came back. And again. Each visit, the group grew - not because anyone recruited them, but because word spread. Someone believed in them.

The Person

Harry
Maurice.

Harry is not a public figure. He is not a career philanthropist. He is a man who showed up in Uganda and chose not to stop. His is not a grand gesture - it is a quiet, consistent practice of belief in young Ugandans.

Two of the young people he mentored - Fatu and Samuel - are now building this foundation around his approach.

"Uganda needs doctors like you, Samuel. You can do this."

- Harry Maurice, to Samuel, one afternoon in Kampala

"Fatu, I am proud of you."

- Harry Maurice, to Fatu
The Co-Founders

Fatu and Samuel
built his legacy.

Two of Harry's original 20 students - Fatu and Samuel - were changed by what one man's consistent belief did for them. They decided that more young people across Uganda deserved the same experience.

So they built it themselves. The Harry Maurice Foundation is their creation, their responsibility, and their tribute. They run every programme, mentor every student, and carry the work forward.

"He showed up for us when no one else did. We want more young people across Uganda to know what that feels like."

Fatu & Samuel — Co-Founders, Harry Maurice Foundation

The Diane Health Programme

Named for the woman
who showed up.

Diane is Harry's sister. She didn't just hear about the work - she came. Visit after visit, she walked into communities and saw that health wasn't just about medicine. It was about access, awareness, and feeling like your wellbeing mattered.

The Diane Health Programme carries her name because she earned it - through presence, not titles.

"Wellness is not a privilege. It is the ground every person stands on."

- The philosophy of the Diane Health Programme
What We Stand On

Four beliefs.
One foundation.

01
Responsibility Within Communities

Grand gestures fade. Consistent presence builds. We show up, repeatedly.
