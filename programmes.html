Mentorship Programme

Roots
& Rise.

Roots & Rise connects young Ugandans with mentors who bring not just advice, but genuine, consistent presence. Named for what it does: it grounds people in their own identity - then helps them rise from it.

We believe mentorship is not a curriculum. It's a relationship. A mentor in Roots & Rise commits time, attention, and belief - and in return, a young person discovers what they are capable of.

DirectionClarity on goals and decisions
ConfidenceBelief in their own worth
NetworksConnections beyond their circle
SkillsPractical communication and planning
Apply to join
How It Works

The Roots & Rise process.

01
Match & Meet
Each participant is matched with a mentor whose experience connects with their goals. The relationship begins with a structured first meeting.
02
Build & Explore
Regular sessions focused on goals and challenges. No rigid curriculum - just honest conversation and consistent attention.
03
Rise & Ripple
Graduates are invited to give back - as peer supporters, ambassadors, and eventually mentors themselves.
Health Programme

Diane Health
Programme.

Named after Diane - Harry's sister who has walked alongside this work from the very beginning - this programme brings primary health education directly into communities and schools.

This isn't about treating illness. It's about preventing it. About helping communities understand their bodies, their choices, and their right to basic wellness.

Health LiteracyUnderstanding self-care
Nutrition AwarenessEveryday wellbeing guidance
Mental WellbeingEmotional health conversations
Community OutreachSessions brought directly to you

Delivery Model

How the Diane Programme reaches people.

01
Community Identification
We work with local leaders to identify communities with limited health resources and awareness.
02
In-Community Sessions
Small, accessible sessions in schools, halls, and open spaces - designed for all ages and backgrounds.
03
Local Champions
We train community members to continue the conversation long after our sessions end - self-sustaining health awareness.
